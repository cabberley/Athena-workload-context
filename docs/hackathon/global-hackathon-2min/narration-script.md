# Clipchamp narration script

**Voice:** confident and neutral

**Pace:** moderate, approximately 135-145 words per minute

**Pronunciation:** Athena = "uh-THEE-nuh"

**Delivery:** respect the pause cues; do not read scene headings

## Timed script

### 0:00-0:09

Every Azure incident starts with signals. But signals alone do not explain what the failure means
for the workload.

### 0:09-0:24

Operators must connect resources, dependencies, changes, telemetry, architecture decisions, and
runbooks before they can judge impact or act safely. That context is fragmented, and every minute
of uncertainty matters.

### 0:24-0:39

Athena is a customer-hosted workload-intelligence and operational-judgment layer for Azure. Azure
MCP establishes what exists and what is happening. Athena explains what it means for this workload,
environment, and organisation.

### 0:39-0:57

Athena records approved workload intent: resource roles, required dependencies, service
objectives, architecture constraints, compensating controls, and accepted risks. The same Azure
evidence can therefore produce different, correct conclusions for Production, Development, and
Training.

### 0:57-1:17

Here, a controlled fault affects the live demonstration workload. Athena identifies the affected
role, reports the contained web-tier impact, raises operator attention, and binds every judgment
to the exact approved context and current Azure evidence.

### 1:17-1:36

It correlates health, topology, network, monitoring, and change evidence. Hypotheses are ranked
deterministically, with supporting evidence, contradictions, missing confirmation, and confidence.
When evidence is stale or ambiguous, Athena fails closed instead of inventing certainty.

### 1:36-1:49

Where approved guidance is available, Athena presents the verified, versioned guidance. If it is
unavailable, Athena says so explicitly. It proposes and explains; it never auto-remediates. When
the workload is restored, Athena independently verifies recovery.

### 1:49-1:57

For Azure teams and customers, that means a path to **[TARGET OUTCOME]**. Athena turns fragmented
Azure signals into trusted workload decisions.

## Clipchamp assembly

1. Generate one voice segment per timed section.
2. Use a short pause after "Athena" and before the final sentence.
3. If the generated voice exceeds 1:54, increase pace slightly rather than removing the customer,
   safety, or proof statements.
4. Replace **[TARGET OUTCOME]** only with an approved measured result or clearly labelled target.
