# Clipchamp narration script

**Voice:** confident and neutral

**Pace:** moderate, approximately 135-145 words per minute

**Pronunciation:** Athena = "uh-THEE-nuh"

**Delivery:** executive, calm, and evidence-led; do not read scene headings

## Timed script

### 0:00-0:12

Azure understands resources, health signals, and changes. But it does not automatically understand
the workload those resources create. We built Athena to supply that missing meaning.

### 0:12-0:30

During an incident, product teams and customer SREs must correlate monitoring, topology, network
evidence, configuration changes, architecture decisions, service objectives, and operational
knowledge. Each system contributes evidence, but the operator still has to reconstruct business
impact and decide which guidance is safe.

### 0:30-0:48

Athena is a customer-hosted workload-intelligence and operational-judgment layer. It maintains
approved workload identity, resource roles, required and prohibited dependencies, environment
profiles, objectives, constraints, compensating controls, accepted risks, ownership, and runbooks.
Declared, observed, inferred, and exception relationships remain separate.

### 0:48-1:08

That context changes the answer. Here, the same Azure topology and evidence run through the same
policy code. Production requires zone resilience. Development accepts reduced scale. Training is
intentionally constrained. Athena preserves the real singleton database risk without recommending
an unsupported architecture.

### 1:08-1:31

The proof then moves to a live Azure-hosted workload. A controlled component fault is detected.
Athena identifies the affected workload role, reports a contained web-tier impact, raises operator
attention, and preserves the evidence lineage. When the workload is restored, Athena verifies
recovery against the same incident and context.

### 1:31-1:52

Athena correlates health, topology, network, monitoring, and change evidence. It produces bounded,
deterministically ranked hypotheses with supporting evidence, contradictions, missing
confirmation, and confidence. Unknown, stale, conflicting, or ambiguous evidence fails closed.

### 1:52-2:12

Operational knowledge is governed rather than generated casually during the incident. Judgments
bind to exact approved context. Verified guidance is presented when available, and unavailable
guidance is explicit. Athena proposes and explains; it never auto-remediates. Customer context and
detailed evidence stay within the customer's Azure boundary.

### 2:12-2:30

Athena complements existing Azure capabilities. Azure MCP establishes what exists and what is
happening. Monitoring, Resource Health, Advisor, and product systems remain authoritative evidence
sources. Athena adds the reusable layer that explains what their evidence means for a specific
workload.

### 2:30-2:48

Epic on Azure is our high-stakes proving ground, not the product boundary. A bounded pilot with
**[AZURE PRODUCT GROUP]** would validate onboarding, evidence integration, judgment quality, and
**[APPROVED OPERATIONAL OUTCOME]** against a second workload architecture.

### 2:48-2:56

We are asking for an Azure product pilot, integration partnership, and access to approved
operational evidence. Athena turns Azure signals into trusted workload decisions.

## Clipchamp assembly

1. Generate one voice segment per timed section.
2. Pause briefly after "missing meaning", "That context changes the answer", and "fails closed".
3. Keep the final ask measured and direct.
4. Replace bracketed text only after the pilot candidate and evidence owner approve it.
