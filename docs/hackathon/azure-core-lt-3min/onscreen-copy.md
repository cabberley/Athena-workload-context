## Claim boundaries

- **Demonstrated:** contextual policy, three-profile proof, verified incident lifecycle, contained
  web-tier impact, ranked hypotheses, guidance verification paths, and recovery verification.
- **To validate:** generalization to a second Azure product architecture and measurable operational
  outcomes.
- **Not claimed:** automatic remediation, production adoption, PR-time prevention, or unmeasured
  cost and reliability improvements.
